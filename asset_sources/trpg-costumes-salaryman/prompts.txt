===== salaryman_nico.png =====
Full-body character illustration of a tabletop-RPG role costume for a Japanese visual-novel game. One person only: the man in reference 1 (Mr. Tekkamen, a 41-year-old Japanese office worker and the quiet regular of a shisha cafe), now dressed as the storybook fantasy character he is role-playing.
Keep his identity exactly from reference 1: black hair slicked back with the same parting and silhouette, thick straight dark eyebrows, narrow half-lidded tired dark eyes, straight nose, firm jaw, light tan skin, no glasses, no beard or stubble, the same lean adult build and body proportions. Expression: deadpan and calm, mouth a closed straight line (he is known for never showing emotion). Not smiling, not younger, not prettier. Do not copy his business suit, shirt or necktie.
Take the outfit from reference 2: copy its garment shapes, colors, materials, props and accessories as closely as possible, refitted to his body. Do not copy that wearer's face, red-and-green hair, amber eyes or build. Reference 3 is an approved picture from this same costume set, in which a different regular wears another character's role costume while keeping his own face; match its drawing style, line weight, shading, earthy palette, background color and full-body framing, but not its outfit or props.
Composition: vertical canvas (2:3 or 3:4, at least 1024x1536). The whole figure from the top of the hair to the shoe soles is visible and centered, about 90% of the canvas height, with small even margins; nothing is cropped. A natural relaxed standing pose with the weight on one leg and a slight three-quarter turn, as in reference 2. Clean, natural hands with five fingers.
Background: one flat solid cream color #EFE4CF from edge to edge. No gradient, no floor line, no cast or contact shadow, no vignette, no frame, no scenery.
No text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures, watermarks or pseudo-writing anywhere, including labels, embroidery and buttons. No hookah or shisha equipment of any kind (no bowl, hose, coals or heat management device), no smoke, no cigarette.
STYLE STANDARD (shisha-game cast): draw this character in the same artist's hand as the approved Minto, Ageha and Rio sprites. Japanese 2D visual-novel game art; confident dark outlines, slightly heavier on the outer silhouette and thinner inside; flat local colors with two to three clearly shaped cel-shadow steps; highlights only as a few deliberate bands on the hair and small catchlights in the eyes. No skin gloss, no airbrush gradients, no rim light or glow, no photographic texture, no pore or fabric micro-detail, no dense ornamental noise, no hundreds of thin hair strands. Limited palette: hair and skin plus three or four main colors. Keep this character's own eye, brow, nose and mouth shapes and natural age and gender differences; do not drift toward a generic AI pretty face.
HEAD-TO-BODY STANDARD: the head from the top of the hair (not counting hats, hoods or ornaments) to the chin must be about one seventh of the full height from the top of the hair to the shoe soles (about 7 heads tall). For the petite cast (Tsumugi, Mashiro, Ageha, Same-chan) it is about 5.8 heads tall instead.
For this costume set, the attached approved costume pictures (references 2 and 3) are the style target. Keep embroidery and patterns as simple and readable as in reference 2, never denser.

OUTFIT (as in reference 2): a cream band-collar linen shirt with the sleeves rolled up to the elbows; a dark olive-green waistcoat with five brass buttons, two lower welt pockets and a patch pocket on his left chest (screen right) holding a small pair of plain metal tongs that hold nothing; dark brown pleated trousers; a brown leather belt; brown leather lace-up shoes. Hands empty and relaxed at his sides. Do not copy the white smudge on the sleeve in reference 2.
