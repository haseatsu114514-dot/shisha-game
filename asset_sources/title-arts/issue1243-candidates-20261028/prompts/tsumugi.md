# tsumugi 生成プロンプト（Claude Opus 設計・DESIGN.md 抜粋）

## 共通スタイル指定

```
Warm anime-style illustration matching a cozy slice-of-life visual novel: clean confident linework, soft painterly cel shading, gentle warm lighting with soft bloom, calm friendly everyday atmosphere, rich but soft color palette. Vertical portrait composition, aspect ratio 2:3 (1024x1536), designed as a title-screen key visual: one single character is the clear, large focal point (figure fills roughly 60–80% of the frame height, face clearly readable), background softly detailed with gentle depth of field. Exactly one person in the image; no other people, no crowds, no silhouettes of bystanders. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or readable signage; all packaging, paper, screens and signs are blank or blurred. Wholesome, modest framing; no suggestive posing. High quality, detailed face and hands, correct anatomy.
Negative: text, lettering, logo, watermark, signature, extra people, crowd, extra fingers, deformed hands, blurry face, nsfw, revealing clothing, pixel art, photorealistic, 3d render.
```

各プロンプトの末尾にも、上の要点（画風・2:3・一人だけ・文字なし・健全）を短くまとめて入れてあるので、プロンプト単体で生成できます。

## tsumugi（つむぎ）

### 1. lavender_field
- コンセプト: 初夏のラベンダー畑。一房を鼻先に寄せて目を閉じる。首にはヘッドホン。
- 狙い: 好きな香りに包まれて、寡黙な彼女の表情がゆるむ瞬間。
- プロンプト:
```
A 21-year-old Japanese art student, introverted and quiet: black bob-cut hair with bangs slightly covering her eyes, pale complexion, wearing a loose cardigan over a simple plain dress, headphones resting around her neck. Early summer afternoon in a gently rolling lavender field, she holds a small sprig of lavender close to her nose with eyes softly closed, a tiny peaceful smile, breeze lifting her bangs, soft purple field and pale blue sky with a few clouds. Thigh-up three-quarter view, she fills the vertical frame. Warm anime-style illustration, soft painterly cel shading, gentle warm sunlight, airy calm mood, lavender purple and soft green palette. Vertical 2:3 portrait 1024x1536, she is the only person in the image, no text, letters or logos anywhere, modest outfit.
```

In a bright university painting studio with tall windows, easels and paint jars, she stands holding a brush, stepping back from a large canvas that shows a soft, dreamy painting of a cat with leaf-shaped ears and a tail made of drifting smoke. She looks at it with quiet, intense focus. Three-quarter back-side view turning slightly toward the viewer, her face visible, thigh-up framing. Warm anime-style illustration, soft painterly cel shading, soft diffused daylight, calm creative mood. Vertical 2:3 portrait 1024x1536, she is the only person in the image, no text, letters, signatures or logos anywhere.
