Create one polished six-frame storyboard mockup of a NEW ORIGINAL ABSTRACT POP-SMOKE SCENE TRANSITION for the Japanese illustrated adventure game 水煙前線 -EN:CODE-.

Art direction: graphic design intelligence and rhythmic impact inspired broadly by stylish Japanese game UI such as Persona 4 and 5, but this design is its own identity. Use a smoke motif transformed into ABSTRACT COLOR SHAPES. Smoke is communicated by big curling rounded plumes, tightly coiled arcs, flat ribbons and a few drifting round lobes. This is ARTISTIC FLAT POP GRAPHICS, not realistic fog or a soft atmospheric smoke simulation. No black-and-gold or luxury restrictions.

Palette: vivid aqua/cyan, juicy coral/orange-pink, warm ivory, with a small amount of plum-purple for thick clean contour lines. Have real confidently saturated color and a large quiet cream region at the peak. Graphic vocabulary: thick flowing contour lines, chunky cropped spirals, bold off-center overlapping plumes, selective large halftone patches and tactile screen-print grain. Energetic curved flow, expansive rounded silhouettes, very clear hierarchy. Avoid generic cartoons of little clouds, a wallpaper of repeated icons, or fragile thin line art.

The two attached game images are ONLY the BEFORE scene (warm lounge) and AFTER scene (night city). They do not constrain the graphic mask palette. Visible portions of the scenes retain their own real colors. The transition is a separate graphic mask over those scenes.

LAYOUT: one landscape concept board, TWO ROWS and THREE equal storyboard panels per row, six panels total. Slim warm-ivory gutters, no headings, no labels. Read left to right across the top row then left to right across the bottom row. Every frame shows the SAME coherent family of shapes moving progressively, rather than six unrelated illustrations:
1 TOP LEFT: lounge is about 80% visible, the first large cyan rounded smoke lobe curls in from the lower left with a coral inner curl.
2 TOP MIDDLE: the same graphic smoke plume swells and rolls clockwise diagonally toward the upper right, about 45% of the lounge remains visible. Thick ivory contours and a small purple halftone area make the layers crisp.
3 TOP RIGHT: lobes join into one huge swooping spiral, only a small clearly shaped window of lounge remains in the upper-right. The mask's center is solid color, not transparent haze.
4 BOTTOM LEFT: FULL OPAQUE COVER. A beautiful punchy abstract pop composition of cyan/coral/ivory curling smoke shapes fills the entire panel; the original scene must be completely absent. A giant offset coil and two large plume lobes give a memorable recognizable peak pose. No scenic image inside this frame. This is the precise background-swap point.
5 BOTTOM MIDDLE: the same curl peels outward to the upper right and lower left, unveiling at least half of the night city. Motion feels like stretching, compressing and springing loose, not just fading opacity.
6 BOTTOM RIGHT: most of the night city is fully visible; a few large cropped plume ends are leaving at the upper right and lower left, maintaining the same graphic colors. A satisfying clean ending.

Crisp contour edges, expressive curved asymmetry, bold flat color surfaces, subtle paper-print texture. Minimal particle noise. Show graphic smoke morphing, with a snappy playful sense of timing and shape design. NO realistic smoke, photoreal fog, 3D rendering, neon glow, fire, portal rings, metallic gold, charcoal packaging, hookah props, characters, stars, confetti, text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI controls or title screens. This is a STATIC visual storyboard proposal, not a runtime sprite sheet or a finished animation. Opaque image background.
