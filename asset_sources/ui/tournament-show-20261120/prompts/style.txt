Style: gala-night stage graphics for the tournament broadcast of a stylish Japanese shisha-bar visual novel, drawn in a clean pixel-painterly anime-game style and used as game UI parts. Deep night purple (#150d22 ground, #221534 panels, #2e1d45 cards) remains the base colour; rich satin gold (#fff6dc highlights, #f6d68e and #e3bd6e body, #b98c3a shadow) is the lead accent and is used generously for trims, rims and ornaments; soft lavender light (#c9a7e6) and a little warm white sparkle are secondary. Luxurious, festive and theatrical like a televised award ceremony: art-deco fan and sunburst motifs, elegant smoke-curl flourishes, tiny star sparkles, glossy highlights and soft bloom. Crisp readable silhouettes, clean edges, front-facing, no perspective distortion, no photo realism. No people, no characters, no hands. No shisha pipes, hookahs, bowls, hoses or charcoal. Never bake in any text, letters, kanji, logo, digits, gauges, icons, hearts or watermark.
