Style: the "paper goods" of a bright daytime shisha shop in a Japanese pixel-painterly visual novel, used as game UI parts for a shisha-making workbench screen. The same warm cream paper as the game's shop screen (#f7f3ed), lavender (#69497e) lines and fills, and amber (#d8963f) only for the one special accent. Keep fine hand-drawn paper fiber and faint print grain. Flat, front-facing, straight lines, no perspective, no photo realism, no 3D rendering, no drop shadows, no strong gradients, no neon, no gold, no brass. Decoration is limited to thin lines and small smoke curls; no medieval ornaments, gears, jewels, lace, ribbons or flowers.
Never bake in any text, letters, logo, digits, gauges, hearts, icons or watermark.
