Style: the "paper goods" of a bright daytime shisha shop in a Japanese pixel-painterly visual novel. The same warm cream paper as the game's shop screen (#f7f3ed), with lavender (#69497e) lines and fills, and only a touch of amber (#d8963f). Keep fine hand-drawn paper fiber and faint print grain. Avoid photo realism, 3D rendering, perspective, drop shadows, strong gradients and neon. Decoration is limited to thin lines and small smoke curls; no medieval ornaments, gears, jewels, lace, ribbons or flowers.
Never bake in any text, letters, logo, digits, gauges, hearts, icons or watermark.

Create ONE flat, front-facing FULL-SCREEN MENU BACKGROUND. The cream paper (#f7f3ed) FILLS THE ENTIRE IMAGE EDGE TO EDGE: no background, no table, no shadow, no visible paper edge, no perspective. A thin lavender (#69497e) double-line frame runs parallel to all four image edges at about 2% inset. Inside the frame, the LEFT 18% of the width is a vertical band tinted very pale lavender (#efe9f0) with a single thin lavender line on its right edge, for a column of tabs. In the LOWER LEFT corner of that band, a small hand-drawn lavender line drawing of a shisha bowl seen from the side (a small clay bowl with a short metal post on top, NOT a cup) with a soft smoke curl rising from it, very light (about 20% strength), within the bottom 30% of the band. The rest of the sheet (right 80%) is plain, nearly uniform cream with fine paper fiber and faint print grain, so cards and lists placed over it stay readable. Straight lines, no other decoration.
